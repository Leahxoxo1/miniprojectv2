— POPULAR TOURIST SPOTS —

Robinsons Mall

Robinsons Mall San Nicolas is a premier shopping destination located in the bustling town of San Nicolas, Ilocos Norte. Situated at the 2nd Level Expansion SAN NICOLAS, our mall offers a wide range of retail and dining options for shoppers of all ages. With a mix of local and international brands, Robinsons Mall provides a unique shopping experience that caters to the diverse needs and tastes of our customers.

Old Plaza

is a historic center known for its colonial-era architecture, red-brick pavement, and lush greenery. Surrounded by historically and culturally significant landmarks, it serves as a central point for both the town's history and its modern life.

Balay San Nicolas

Balay San Nicolas, located west of the town plaza, had stood witness to the town’s evolution, though its last key role in the affairs of the municipality was as the headquarters of the Japanese army during World War II. Although it was pretty much left to fend for itself against the ravages of time for most of its history, there is no denying it is of particular significance as a cultural, artistic, and historical gem.

Santa Rosa Academy Building

Sta. Rosa Academy of San Nicolas, Ilocos Norte, Inc. is a Catholic educational institution that provides quality education and instills Christian values. As part of the Diocese of Laoag Catholic Educational System, it is located next to the historic San Nicolas Catholic Church, which was built in 1701.

Diocesan Shrine and Parish of San Nicolas de Tolentino

The Diocesan Shrine and Parish of San Nicolás de Tolentino, formerly known as San Nicolas de Tolentino Parish Church, is a Roman Catholic parish church located in the municipality of San Nicolas, Ilocos Norte, Philippines. It is under the jurisdiction of the Diocese of Laoag. Exhibiting a baroque façade, the church bears the Spanish coat of arms. A three-storey belltower on its right was built by Father Vitoriano Garcia.

San Nicolas Banga

a significant symbol of craftsmanship and tradition in San Nicolas, Ilocos Norte, known for its pottery industry called alfareria. These iconic clay pots are historically used as water and food containers, with each one reflecting the artisan's unique skill. The creation of bangas is a generational labor of love, and their importance is celebrated through events like the Damili Festival in San Nicolas

— Authentic Local Creations —

Ilocos Empanada

A popular Ilocano snack made with rice flour, filled with meat, vegetables, and egg, often enjoyed with vinegar.

Chichacorn

A crunchy snack made from fried corn kernels, typically seasoned with garlic and salt.

Inabel Fabric

A traditional woven textile characterized by intricate patterns and vibrant colors. It's used for clothing, home decor, and more.

Basi Wine

A local fermented sugarcane drink that is a staple in the region, often enjoyed during celebrations.

Ilocos Longganisa

A flavorful local sausage made primarily from pork, known for its distinct taste and spices.

San Nicolas Pottery

Local artisans create beautiful pottery, often using traditional techniques that have been passed down through generations.

Bayan ng San Nicolas
